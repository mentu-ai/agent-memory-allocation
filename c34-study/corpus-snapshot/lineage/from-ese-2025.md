# Lineage: from the 2025 ESE corpus

The predecessor corpus lives at `Workspaces/epistemic-main` (with earlier strata in
`Workspaces/mentu-physics` and public essays in `Workspaces/rashidazarang/blog`). It
proposed Epistemic Science & Engineering as a field: six scientific and four
engineering disciplines, ~129 "laws," a canon of foundational documents, and a
deployed platform (epistemicscience.org, last active mid-2025).

A full-corpus review (2026-06-10) judged it theory-rich and empirically ungrounded:
laws asserted without measurement procedures, parameters (r, S, M, I) defined only
through each other, validation self-referential, and heavy redundancy (three versions
of the acceleration law, overlapping thermodynamics suites). Its strongest material —
the Structure–Memory–Interaction triad, substrate theory, return-as-intelligence,
friction-to-production, and the operationalizable physics analogies (mass, entropy,
impedance, momentum) — descends into this corpus as conjectures.

## Concepts retained as vocabulary (not claims)

- **Structure–Memory–Interaction triad** (`canon/foundational-principles/epistemic-triad.md`)
  — used as an organizing frame; it is a definition, not a testable claim, so it
  carries no status.
- **Substrate theory** (`canon/foundational-documents/epistemic-substrate-theory-canonical-source.md`)
  — "architecture determines what knowledge can exist and evolve" motivates the whole
  program; individual consequences of it enter as conjectures.
- **The Epistemic Signal** (`engine/foundations/cir-memory-as-infrastructure.md`)
  — no longer theory at all: implemented in mentu-engine's CIR (schema v14). See
  `instruments/mentu-instrument.md`.
