# Exclusions

What the 2025 corpus contained that this corpus does not admit, and why. Nothing here
was silently dropped; nothing here is forbidden forever. The bar for entry is the
constitution's: a conceivable measurement procedure.

## 1. Consciousness scoring (RIP-6, the five-thresholds model)

- **Source**: `epistemic-main/canon/foundational-documents/rip-6-consciousness-emergence.md`,
  `canon/theoretical-positions/five-thresholds-consciousness-model.md`
- **Claimed**: a system measured at "77.7% consciousness" across five weighted
  thresholds, with "meta-delusion protection."
- **Why excluded**: the scores have no computation procedure independent of the system
  scoring itself; no operationalization distinguishes "consciousness" from recursive
  self-modeling; the validation was internal to the platform making the claim. This
  was the 2025 corpus's largest credibility cost.
- **Readmission condition**: an externally checkable operational definition. None is
  on the horizon; this is effectively retired.

## 2. Quantum laws of epistemic indeterminacy

- **Source**: `canon/law-suites/quantum-laws-of-epistemic-indeterminacy.md`
- **Why excluded**: no procedure decides when an idea is "in superposition" versus
  merely ambiguous; no probability amplitudes are assignable. The analogy borrows
  prestige without generating predictions.

## 3. Relativistic laws of epistemic frame theory

- **Source**: `canon/law-suites/relativistic-laws-of-epistemic-frame-theory.md`
- **Why excluded**: frame-dependence of interpretation is real but the Lorentz
  machinery adds nothing measurable; no transformation can be computed. The plain
  observation ("different agents read the same signal differently") is already
  covered by per-actor segmentation in analyses.

## 4. Numeric thresholds asserted without derivation

- **Examples**: F/P ≤ 0.5 (acceleration law), irreversibility threshold I_c
  (thermodynamics suite).
- **Why excluded as numbers**: the constants were never derived or fitted. The
  *directional* claims survive as C2 and C3; if the data exhibits real thresholds,
  they will be estimated from it, not asserted.

## 5. Epistemic temperature

- **Source**: `canon/law-suites/zeroth-law-of-epistemic-thermodynamics.md`
- **Why excluded**: defined only as "activity intensity"; every other retained claim
  can be stated without it. If a future analysis needs a temperature-like quantity,
  it must define one from fields that exist.

## 6. Self-reported platform metrics as evidence

- **Examples**: "351 components, 89.3% confidence, integration_health 1.0" (README of
  epistemic-main).
- **Why excluded**: internal counters of a system are inventory, not validation.
  This corpus's results must derive from operational outcome data with stated
  confounders.

## 7. Escape-velocity threshold

- **Source**: `canon/foundational-documents/the-law-of-epistemic-acceleration.md`
  (the "critical point beyond which epistemic growth becomes self-sustaining").
- **Metabolized**: 2026-06-21 weekly synthesis (`observatory/packets/2026-06-21.md`),
  the dependent sibling of the multiplicative acceleration law metabolized 2026-06-14.
- **Why not admitted as a separate conjecture**: two parts, neither of which stands
  alone. (a) The *numeric* threshold value was never derived or fitted — it joins the
  §4 family of asserted-not-fitted constants. (b) Its *qualitative* content is not an
  independent claim: "self-sustaining growth" is exactly the region of **C6**'s
  parameter space where the fitted state-proportional growth coefficient k ≥ 1. There
  is nothing left to test that C6 does not already test.
- **Readmission condition**: subsumed into C6. If C6 graduates with a fitted growth
  coefficient, escape velocity is **read off that fit** (does any matured stratum show
  k ≥ 1?) — never asserted as a standalone number, and never as a separate file.

## 8. Wave / oscillation / resonance suites

- **Source**: the 2025 corpus's wave/oscillation/resonance law family (plausible
  physics metaphors carried in `from-ese-2025.md`'s "deliberately not yet admitted"
  list).
- **Metabolized**: 2026-06-28 weekly synthesis (`observatory/packets/2026-06-28.md`).
- **Why not admitted as a conjecture**: two parts, neither of which clears the bar.
  (a) The *resonance / amplitude / phase / standing-wave* machinery has no measurement
  procedure on Mentu data — there is no operational definition of an epistemic "natural
  frequency" at which signals are "amplified," and no field assigns phase or amplitude.
  It borrows physics prestige without generating a prediction, joining the quantum (§2)
  and relativistic (§3) suites. (b) The one genuinely measurable residue —
  *periodicity / oscillation in an operational time series* — needs no "wave law": it is
  directly testable by autocorrelation / spectral analysis of run cadence whenever a
  specific periodicity question arises, and its most salient instance — scheduling
  cadence that rises and falls without tracking the failure ledger (the `ane-fortress`
  whipsaw 32→47→21→5→5→2) — is already captured by **C24 P2** (cadence decoupling).
  There is nothing left to test that a direct spectral analysis or C24 does not already
  cover.
- **Readmission condition**: a field that assigns a measurable phase, amplitude, or
  natural frequency to an epistemic signal, OR a concrete periodicity claim that cannot
  be answered by direct spectral analysis of an existing time series. Neither is on the
  horizon. Note this does **not** forbid measuring periodicity itself — it forbids
  dressing that measurement as a borrowed wave/resonance law.

## 9. Meta-cognitive-architectures series

- **Source**: `science/meta-cognitive-architectures/` in the 2025 corpus — ADHD as an
  epistemic architecture, attention-surface geometry, neurotype-matched knowledge
  interfaces (carried in `from-ese-2025.md`'s "deliberately not yet admitted" list as
  "parked, not judged").
- **Metabolized**: 2026-07-13 weekly synthesis, catch-up for the missed Sundays 07-05
  and 07-12 (`observatory/packets/2026-07-13.md`).
- **Why not admitted as a conjecture**: the claims are about a population the
  instrument does not observe. Mentu records operational events of agent runs and
  commitment lifecycles; it contains no human-cognition measurements — no neurotype
  data, no human task-switching telemetry, no attention traces. No operationalization
  against this instrument exists even in principle (constitution rule 6). Unlike the
  wave suites (§8), whose one measurable residue was already carried by C24 P2, no
  residue survives here that any open conjecture does not already carry: the series'
  interface-design implications (progressive disclosure, context-surface shaping) are
  design heuristics, not claims this corpus can test.
- **Readmission condition**: an instrument that measures human cognition directly
  (consented human task/attention telemetry with a defined schema), which this corpus
  does not contemplate. Until then the series stays excluded — parked→excluded, not
  judged false.
