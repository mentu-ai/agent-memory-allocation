# Audit of the external agent's critique
*Positioning memo, 2026-07-02 — not a corpus artifact. Companion to `contribution-strategy-memo.md`.*

An external agent (no repo access) reviewed the published-facing material and offered ten
"hidden" insights plus an umbrella reframing. This memo audits each claim against what
actually exists in the canon (`Workspaces/epistemic-main`), this corpus, and Mentu.
Headline: **of ten points, ~six already exist here (three operationalized, two excluded
with documented reasons, one as vocabulary), two are genuinely additive, and two are
seductive regressions the constitution already immunizes against.** The most valuable
property of the critique is convergence: an independent mind re-derived both this
program's best moves and the 2025 corpus's worst temptations. That is calibration data,
not revelation — and it validates the exclusions ledger as real technology.

## The umbrella question

> *"What are the necessary and sufficient conditions for a knowledge system to improve
> itself faster than entropy degrades it?"*

**Adopt — with provenance noted.** This is the R₀ > 1 self-sustainment condition (return
rate × value per return > decay + maintenance cost) restated as a field-defining question,
and it is nearly this repo's README thesis. Three independent formulations now converge on
it (the 2025 canon poetically, this corpus operationally, the external agent rhetorically).
Its work: **epistemic acceleration becomes one phenomenon, structural waste one failure
mode, the ledger one instrument, and Mentu the experimental platform rather than the
theory.** This is the correct spine for the eventual field-naming essay — after the
anchors, per the sequencing strategy.

## Amendments adopted into the program

1. **Decision-level outcomes** (from #9): extend `mentu.epistemic_handle.v1` with
   decision-linkage fields; C25's secondary outcome becomes "returns that altered a
   subsequent commitment," not merely access.
2. **Phase diagram** (from #2): the formal threshold model gets staged, orderable
   thresholds; cross-system work tests stage-skipping as the falsifier.
3. **Named central question** (umbrella): add one line to the README; touches no frozen
   prediction or verdict.
4. **Protocol paper** (from #5): planned as a deliverable class alongside the science.
5. **Conservation bookkeeping** (from #8): adopted as a ledger completeness check.
6. **Entropy literalism** (#4) and **wave-family temptations** stay in `lineage/exclusions.md`
   with their existing readmission bounties. The external critique's independent arrival at
   §4's exact temptation is itself evidence the exclusions ledger is load-bearing.

## Meta-finding

Every reviewer of this work — human, internal agent, external agent — is pulled toward the
same two attractors: the *good* one (self-sustainment threshold + instrumentation-first)
and the *bad* one (grand physics analogies asserted as laws). The corpus's constitution is
precisely the technology that separates them. The critique changed the program at the
margins (two adoptions, one naming, one deliverable class) and changed the theory not at
all — which is what a healthy research program looks like when reviewed.
