# C26b — Search-reachability as the first-order return variable

## Claim

The first-order determinant of whether a stored knowledge object is ever
exercised is **search-reachability** — whether its content is directly
reachable by associative search (grep/glob over plain files) from the agent's
working context — not residency depth (C26's registered ordering) and not
annotation richness (C7's mechanism). Among non-boot-resident objects,
greppable-content objects (T2) are exercised at rates at least comparable to,
and predicted here to exceed, pointer-resident objects (T1) whose content is
reachable only by following a resident pointer; both dwarf tool-gated objects
(T3). Access is overwhelmingly mediated — a search hit or an in-context
mention precedes it — rather than spontaneous.

This conjecture was **generated by** C26's recorded, non-verdict-bearing
estimates (T2 32.35% vs T1 8.79%, seen at n=160 under a failed coverage
floor). Per the corpus's seen-estimate rule, those estimates are **barred from
adjudicating this claim**: hypothesis-generating data cannot also be
hypothesis-confirming data. C26b is a fresh bet on fresh data.

## Non-amendment declaration

C26 remains registered exactly as frozen (floor 500, ordering T1 > T2 > T3)
and will be adjudicated at its own covered re-run. C26b does not amend,
replace, or re-floor C26. If C26's covered run *supports* its original
ordering, C26b is expected to be refuted, and both verdicts publish.

## Operationalization

**Adjudication corpus (the boundary rule)**: interactive sessions whose first
timestamp is **strictly after this conjecture's registration commit**
(the commit that introduces this file; its hash and timestamp are the
boundary). Sessions before the boundary — including the 2026-07-18 frozen
corpus and the gauge lines accrued between 2026-07-18 and registration — are
excluded from adjudication. Data sources: session transcripts
(`~/.claude/projects/*/*.jsonl`), the boot-manifest gauge
(`~/.claude/telemetry/boot-manifests.jsonl`, excluding `pipe-test-000`), and
memory/workspace file censuses at analysis time; T3 rates imported from
frozen results only. This program's own sessions (those discussing C26b's
subjects) are excluded, as before.

**Tier assignment**: as in C26 (deterministic, in the committed analyzer):
T1 = memory files + skill bodies (pointer-resident); T2 = workspace files
with no boot pointer (paged-searchable); T0 descriptive only; T3 from frozen
results. Objects must predate a session to be eligible for it.

**Primary metric**: ever-exercised fraction per tier over the adjudication
corpus (access = Read/invocation, as in C26 — the same frozen definition, so
predecessor and successor are commensurable). Secondary: median per-object
eligible-session rate; mechanism attribution (mediated vs spontaneous) by
first-in-session appearance, as committed in the C26 analyzer.

**Richness predictor**: C26's anchor score (typed identity, lineage links,
scope, tags, summary; rich ≥3, sparse ≤1), scored on frontmatter at analysis
time with the drift caveat declared.

## Predictions (frozen 2026-07-22, before any adjudication-corpus data exists)

- **P1 (the inversion is real)**: ever-exercised fraction of T2 ≥ **1.5×**
  that of T1 (combined) in the adjudication corpus. (The seen ratio was
  ~3.7×; 1.5× is the frozen bet — regression to parity or reversal
  falsifies.)
- **P2 (mediation)**: ≥ **80%** of T2 exercises have a visible in-session
  search or resident-pointer antecedent; spontaneous access stays the
  minority path.
- **P3 (richness stays second-order)**: within T2, the rich/sparse
  ever-exercised ratio is ≤ **2×** and strictly smaller than the T2/T1 gap.
- **P4 (the cliff endures)**: T2 ever-exercised ≥ **100×** the frozen T3
  baseline (0.0222%), i.e. ≥ 2.22%.

## Falsification criteria

- T1 (combined) ≥ T2 on the adjudication corpus with floors met → **refuted**;
  residency depth reasserts, the seen inversion was an artifact of the
  retroactive corpus, and C26's original ordering stands rehabilitated.
- T2/T1 in (1.0, 1.5) → **revised**: searchability advantage exists but the
  "first-order" language must be withdrawn.
- Mediated fraction < 60% → **refuted on mechanism** (spontaneous recall is a
  real path; reachability is not the gate this claim says it is).
- Rich/sparse ≥ 2× and ≥ the T2/T1 gap → **refuted in favor of C7's
  mechanism** (annotation, not reachability, is first-order).
- Tier assignment or denominators non-deterministic → **instrument
  insufficient**, no verdict.

## Gate

Verdict only from a committed `analyses/c26b-search-reachability-first-order/
analyze.py`, written and committed after this registration and before its
first output, reading only boundary-respecting data. **Coverage floors
(frozen now, chosen at registration for a new conjecture — not an amendment
of C26's 500)**: ≥ **300** post-boundary interactive sessions spanning ≥ 2
month cohorts and ≥ 10 projects; ≥ 100 eligible objects in each of T1 and T2.
Rationale recorded at registration: at ~100 objects per tier, 300 sessions
bound the tier ever-exercised fractions to roughly ±6 pp (95% binomial),
sufficient to separate the frozen 1.5× threshold from parity at the seen
effect sizes' order of magnitude; the number is frozen before any boundary
data exists, which is what makes it legitimate.

## Known limitations

- Single-operator ecosystem (external validity bound carried from the
  program; the paper's replication invitation is the mitigation).
- "Exercised" is access, not utility (instrument note limit 3).
- The gauge now logs per-session resident sets, so T0/T1 membership per
  session is measured rather than assumed for post-boundary sessions — an
  instrument improvement over C26's retroactive assignment, disclosed here
  because it makes the successor's instrument strictly stronger than the
  predecessor's.
- Workflow drift risk: the operator's practices may change over the accrual
  window (including in response to this program's own findings). Any
  deliberate practice change must be registered as a co-intervention marker
  before it ships, per the estate's standing pattern.
