- *Four-factor joint form stays parked (no measurement procedure).* r, S, M, and

  agreed scalar. As stated, the joint law is still un-operationalizable — the

- *Reduced shape becomes a conjecture-candidate (C6 — epistemic compounding).*
  The empirically distinguishing content of "multiplicative" is **state-
  proportional growth**: per-period fractional growth of a workspace's epistemic
  stock is roughly constant (⇒ log E linear in t / ΔE ∝ E), as opposed to
  additive growth (ΔE constant, E linear in t). This *is* measurable on the
  instrument without decomposing r·S·M·I — using the C2 datasets already mapped:
  cumulative approved/closed commitments per workspace per week, or cumulative
  distilled-knowledge signals (`reflection`/`learning`), as the E(t) proxy.
  Distinguish multiplicative from additive by testing whether the per-week
  increment correlates with the level (ΔE ∝ E) and whether log E fits linear vs
  E fits linear. `[conjecture-candidate]` — admission to `corpus/conjectures/`
  still requires the constitution's full bar (frozen E-proxy choice, frozen
  directional prediction, frozen falsification threshold) before any data is
